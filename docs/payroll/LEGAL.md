# Legal roadmap — entity wrapper options (a slide, not advice)

**Not legal advice. No counsel was consulted for this document. Everything here is
jurisdiction-dependent and changes over time; verify with a lawyer before any real wages
flow.** The hackathon prototype runs on testnet with a mock stablecoin — no real
employment, wages, or filings are implicated by building it.

## What the slide says

One line on the roadmap slide: **"Entity wrapper: [selected option] — next step: engage
counsel."** That is the whole legal claim we make on stage. Legal is where this is going,
not something we have done.

## Wrapper options for the roadmap slide

| Option | What it gives | Watch-outs to raise with counsel |
|---|---|---|
| **Wyoming DUNA** (Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association Act, SF0050) | Recognizes DAO-style smart-contract governance; the entity is separate from its members for liability; may engage in profit-making activities in support of its purpose. Signed 2024-03-07, effective 2024-07-01. | Requires at least 100 members — does not fit a small company; nonprofit-purpose framing; US tax exemption is not automatic |
| **Marshall Islands DAO LLC** (MIDAO) | LLC under the 2022 DAO Act; for-profit or nonprofit; can register with one founding member; fits globally distributed teams | Non-US entity: banking access, tax residency and any later conversion need counsel |
| **Swiss association (Verein)** | The structure several major DAOs have adopted; low formality and cost | General orientation only (not verified in this pass); Swiss formalities plus local employment/social-security law still apply |
| **Conventional LLC / C-corp + on-chain payroll overlay** | Employment, tax and banking law fit cleanly; the on-chain vault becomes internal payroll tooling; the simplest path to real wages | The "DAO" story is legally thin: votes bind through the operating agreement, not the chain |
| **Off-chain legal wrapper + on-chain execution** | A real entity holds the keys/contracts the votes control | Two-layer complexity: who is liable when a vote executes is a counsel question |

## What no wrapper solves by itself

Employment law applies regardless of the wrapper: wage-payment rules, minimum wage,
withholding and tax, worker classification, and notice/consent requirements for changing
pay. Treat a vote as an input to a legally effective pay change — not as the change
itself. That is a counsel question, not something we decide in a hackathon.

## Questions to bring to the first counsel meeting

1. Which jurisdiction for the entity, given where the employees actually live?
2. Can a one-person-one-vote on-chain vote be a legally effective amendment to
   compensation terms, or must it route through written consent?
3. How do stablecoin wages interact with wage-payment laws (valuation, timing, records)?
4. Who is the employer of record — the wrapper, the treasury, or an employer-of-record
   service?
5. What do we need so on-chain payroll events are acceptable payroll records?
