Lane 1 · evidence → intent

The four authored documents are hashed into a Merkle root. The pre-baked extractor proposes claims and rules; the validator rejects any claim without a document quote; the reviewer resolves the two ambiguities. The rule set and assumptions produce intentHash, contentHash and actionsHash.

Lane 2 · commitment → chain

A's contracts add the append-only registry, signed disclosure, governor vote and executor. The executor recomputes the commitment and refuses anything that does not match the committed rules. Sentinel simulates and flags before execution.

Lane 3 · execution → report

B's reference executor applies its own rule/commitment model to the same demo. The decoder turns calldata into a recursive action tree, maps custom errors, and produces a deterministic report with the quote, the call, the constraint and the evidence.

Lane 4 · conflicts → decision

Seam 1 · commitment

A and B compute different commitments from different preimages. A binds a typehash and version hash; B binds a proposal key, evidence root and disclosure. This is the first thing the integration must reconcile.

Seam 2 · executor

A's IntentLockExecutor.execute takes rules and actions; B's reference execute takes a three-argument bundle. A deployed call needs an adapter, not a rename.

Seam 3 · evidence

A's evidence store and B's evidence manifest are independent. The roots will not match until one store feeds both.

Seam 4 · truth

A's scorecard says 11/11 scenarios pass. B's scorecard says 9 properties are TEST_REQUIRED because B is not wired to A. Both are true in their own tree; the combined truth is the integration work.
