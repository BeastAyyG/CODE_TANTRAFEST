05 / Intelligence bridge · Person A

Turning documents
into a defensible rule set.

main · intelligence/

Person A's intelligence modules are the bridge between source documents and the chain. They are real code, but two of them disagree with the executor they are meant to feed. That is the most important integration finding in the project.

evidence.ts

Content-addressed store, sorted-pair Merkle root, proofs, and a verifySource that re-hashes the exact file and bounds the quoted span. An empty tree is an explicit unknown, not a zero hash.

realMerkle

chain.ts

Four sequential stages with thresholds in one CONFIG. Missing input yields UNKNOWN, never PASS. Execution is a hard gate: reverted, flagged or halted → RED; pending with a Sentinel violation → RED; not submitted → UNKNOWN.

realUNKNOWN-never-PASS

graph.ts

Simulated wallets, four link types, noisy-OR, disclosed damping and an evidence-bound challenge payload. Like B's graph, it is a risk signal over fake transfers, not a finding of wrongdoing.

simulated datareal math

ai-draft.ts

Produces an IntentSpec; ambiguous questions stay in unresolved[] rather than being guessed. It has heuristic and LLM-shaped paths, but no live model is required to explain the demo.

advisoryunresolved

intent-bridge.ts · divergence 1

It computes intentHash = keccak256(canonical JSON). The executor computes keccak256(abi.encode(rules)). The bridge refuses to run while unresolved[] is non-empty, but once it runs, the hash it produces is not the executor's hash.

real bugbreaks P1

intent-bridge.ts · divergence 2

It reports childCount = children.length for MULTICALL but encodes an empty inner bytes[]. A bridged multicall can never match an on-chain rule set.

real bugbreaks nested enforcement
The bridge is not just plumbing The chain layer and the console agree on the concept of a commitment but not on its preimage, error vocabulary, action encoding or graph constants. Fixing these is the first real integration milestone — before wiring any button to a contract.
