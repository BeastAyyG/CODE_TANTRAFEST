10 / Positioning

Why this is not
just another Governor.

the precise claim

Binding a vote to calldata is already solved by Compound and OpenZeppelin Governors. The honest gap is comprehension, evidence, conflicts and code identity at execution. Say that before a judge says it for you.

SystemIt already hasIt leaves open
Compound / OZ GovernorProposal id = hash of encoded callsDescription is a separate string; no evidence or disclosure commitment
Tally / Tenderly / OZ DefenderDecoding, simulation, state diffsAdvisory display; the executor does not consult it
Zodiac RolesStanding calldata permissions at executionA policy, not a per-proposal commitment
IntentLockPer-proposal commitment + refusal semanticsEvidence and disclosure are in the commitment; the report makes the gap legible

Say these limits out loud

A vote that honestly authorizes something bad still executes. We bind intent to action; we do not judge whether the intent is wise.
Self-authored evidence can be internally consistent. The Merkle root proves the documents did not change after signing; it does not prove they are true.
A compromised governor or executor invalidates the guarantee. Commitment immutability is a security property, not a nicety.
The informed-vote receipt raises the cost of voting blind. It does not stop a delegate voting against their mandate with their eyes open.

The fuller prior-art review and build direction are in person-a-direction.md.
