# AIP-042 — Ecosystem Growth Grant: GrowthLabs × Developer Guild

**Author:** Nova Labs (proposer)
**Posted:** 12 October 2026
**Snapshot:** aegis.forum/t/aip-042
**Requested:** 250,000 USDC from the AegisDAO treasury

## 1. Why now

AegisDAO's ecosystem is growing faster than its grant operations. Two established teams have asked for multi-month funding, and the current one-off proposal process takes three weeks per grant. This proposal funds a single six-month growth program and the tooling that supports it.

## 2. What this proposal funds

- **GrowthLabs — 200,000 USDC (one-time).** GrowthLabs will run a six-month ecosystem growth program: developer onboarding, regional meetups, and a grant-matching pool. Funds are released to the GrowthLabs multisig after the vote and are not milestone-gated at the protocol level.
- **Developer Guild — 50,000 USDC (one-time).** The Developer Guild will maintain contributor tooling, documentation, and the public SDK.
- **TreasuryOps allowance — 5,000 USDC ceiling.** A bounded allowance for review costs, with no other spending authority.

The grant is a one-time payment. It is not a recurring commitment, and no future payment is implied.

## 3. Treasury impact

At today's balance of 1,200,000 USDC and a monthly operating burn of 122,000 USDC, the requested 250,000 USDC leaves approximately 7.8 months of runway. The full model is attached as `financial-model.csv`.

## 4. Delivery

GrowthLabs will publish quarterly milestone reports. The external letter from GrowthLabs describes the program scope and the disbursement plan. The Developer Guild reports through its existing monthly contributor call.

## 5. Implementation note

To support programmatic disbursement at scale, we will align the treasury implementation with the program's delivery roadmap during the grant period. This is an operational alignment, not a change to the treasury's custody model.

## 6. Disclosure

The proposer's disclosed wallets are listed in the attached disclosure. Voting opens after the disclosure window closes. Any undisclosed link between the proposer and a recipient is a material governance risk.

## 7. Voting

This proposal uses the standard 48-hour timelock. It is queued for execution only if the commitment passes and no verified disclosure challenge is open.
